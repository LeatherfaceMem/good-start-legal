Study driving theory and take a practice exam at your own pace.

Get started

Open Tests and choose a numbered test. For a mock exam, open the exam section, choose a licence category, and start an attempt. The Good Start mock exam contains 23 questions and runs for up to 30 minutes.

Language, offline access, and progress

Choose Armenian, Russian, or English in Settings. The interface, driving theory and first-aid questions, psychological exercises, and answer choices are available in the selected language. Bundled questions and diagrams, results, and bookmarks are available offline. You can review completed tests and resume unfinished ones.

Troubleshooting and bug reports

For direct help, email goodstartcontact@gmail.com. Close and reopen Good Start. If the problem continues, contact Good Start support on GitHub. Include the app version, device and OS, selected language, test and question numbers, and the steps to reproduce the problem. Posting requires a GitHub account. Issues are public, so keep personal information out of the report.

Question source

The source for categories A, B, and C is the Armenian Ministry of Internal Affairs question bank. Good Start is an independent educational app and is not an official app of an Armenian government agency.
