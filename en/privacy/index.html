Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026.

About the app

This policy applies to Good Start — Driving School. The project’s public GitHub account is LeatherfaceMem. You can use the app without an account.

What stays on your device

Your answers, unfinished attempts, results, bookmarks, and settings are stored in a local database. These records let you resume studying and review your results. If you enable reminders, the app uses your device time zone and system notifications to remind you at the selected time.

Internet and third parties

Study materials come with the app. The app does not send learning records to the developer and does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. External links open in your browser. GitHub hosts this site and its support channel under its own privacy terms. Public support posts are visible to others.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings → Reset learning progress to remove unfinished attempts, history, and bookmarks. This keeps your app settings. Your device offers controls to remove the app’s local data. Backup and restore depend on your device and system settings.

Security and age

Local data stays within the app’s storage area. Good Start is intended for learners preparing for a driving theory exam. The app does not request a birth date or other age information.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email goodstartcontact@gmail.com. Use the Good Start GitHub support channel for general privacy questions. Keep personal information out of public posts. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
